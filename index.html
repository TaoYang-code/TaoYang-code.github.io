About Me

My research lies at the intersection of civil engineering, digital construction, and artificial intelligence. I obtained my PhD in Civil Engineering from the University of Auckland, New Zealand.

My current research integrates engineering knowledge, point cloud processing, and deep learning to create reliable digital representations of existing infrastructure. I focus on knowledge-guided Scan-to-BIM, including bridge component recognition and point cloud completion, and its extension towards continuous model updating and infrastructure digital twins.

My long-term vision is to develop intelligent, adaptive, and human-supervised infrastructure digital twins that connect engineering models, knowledge, and AI to support inspection, maintenance, and decision-making throughout the infrastructure lifecycle, contributing to a safer and more resilient built environment.

Research Interests

  • Intelligent infrastructure digitalisation: engineering knowledge-guided Scan-to-BIM, point cloud understanding and completion, and continuous model updating using LiDAR, imagery, and AI.
  • Infrastructure digital twins: connecting digital models with inspection information and asset conditions to support assessment, maintenance, and lifecycle management.
  • Knowledge-grounded AI for engineering: AI agents and multi-agent workflows that connect engineering models, standards, records, and analytical tools for human-supervised decision support.
